SMS Consent & Messaging Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

This page explains how Financial Stream LLC uses SMS for customer-initiated, service-related communication.

What SMS Is Used For

  • Missed-call follow-up when you call our business number and we miss your call.
  • One-to-one communication to answer questions or coordinate an appointment after you reply or opt in.
  • Service-related reminders or updates connected to your inquiry.

Your Consent

By calling our business number or submitting a contact form with the SMS checkbox selected, you consent to receive text messages related to your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out / Help

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to financialstreamllc@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
