Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

Financial Stream LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you contact us through the website, email, phone, forms, or SMS.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit, such as name, phone number, email address, and message.
  • Business details you voluntarily share to request bookkeeping, tax, or administrative support.
  • Basic technical data used for website functionality and security.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to requests and provide service-related support.
  • To send service updates or reminders by email or SMS when you request or consent to that communication.
  • To improve service quality and maintain website security.

SMS Communications

If you opt in, we may send service-related SMS only at your request, such as a missed-call follow-up, consultation confirmation, or status update. We do not send marketing blasts. Message frequency varies, and carrier rates may apply. You can opt out by replying STOP or request help by replying HELP.

Sharing and Security

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing. We use reasonable safeguards, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to financialstreamllc@gmail.com.
