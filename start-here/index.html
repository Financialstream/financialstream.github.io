Prepare enough context to route the question clearly.

You do not need everything organized before choosing a path. Start with what you know now, and use the structured request when you are ready to share the situation.

  • Service area or situation
  • Business or personal context
  • Tax year or reporting period
  • Document type or notice type
  • QuickBooks/bookkeeping status
  • Payroll, Sales Tax / DOR, or business setup context if relevant
  • What feels unclear
  • What decision you are trying to make
Where to go next

Use the path that matches your level of clarity.

Start a structured requestBest when you have a specific bookkeeping, tax, payroll, Sales Tax, setup, or document question. Compare servicesBest when you know the general area but want to confirm the service path. Read materials firstBest when you are researching before contacting or preparing documents. Review documents / unclear situationBest when a notice, report, prior filing, or unclear record needs practical review.
Before sending sensitive information

Start with the situation, document types, year or period, and what is unclear. Do not send full SSNs, passwords, account credentials, bank logins, passport scans, full tax documents, or sensitive owner/employee data through unsecured messages.
