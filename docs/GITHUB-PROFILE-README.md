## What we help with

Financial Stream LLC helps U.S. small businesses organize financial records, prepare required information, and move through accounting, payroll, reporting, tax, formation, and document-review work with a clearer process.

The approved production service structure contains six equal core areas:

- **Bookkeeping / QuickBooks** — organization, cleanup, catch-up, reconciliations, and ongoing record support.
- **Tax Returns / Tax Preparation** — preparation support based on the client’s documents and situation.
- **Sales Tax / DOR Reporting** — reporting support for relevant state and local requirements.
- **Payroll / L&I / Quarterly Reporting** — payroll records and related quarterly and L&I reporting support.
- **Business Setup / Company Formation** — formation-related documents and initial financial organization.
- **Financial Consulting / Document Review** — structured review of financial and business records before the appropriate next step is taken.

Payroll is an approved core service and is not pending additional confirmation.

Service scope depends on the client’s facts, documents, jurisdiction, and requested work.

## Who we support

- U.S. small businesses and business owners;
- service-based businesses;
- private clients with tax or financial-document needs;
- clients who need better-organized records and filing information;
- remote service requests across the United States;
- English- and Russian-speaking clients.

## How the process starts

1. Submit a [structured request](https://financialstreamllc.com/contact/#structured-request).
2. Provide the relevant details or documents requested by the form.
3. Financial Stream reviews the situation and requested scope.
4. The client receives an appropriate next step.
