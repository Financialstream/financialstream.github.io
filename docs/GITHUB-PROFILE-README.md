## What we help with

Financial Stream LLC helps U.S. small businesses organize financial records, prepare required information, and move through accounting and tax-related work with a clearer process.

- **Bookkeeping** — organization, cleanup, and ongoing record support.
- **Tax Returns** — preparation support based on the client’s documents and situation.
- **Sales Tax** — reporting support for relevant state and local requirements.
- **Business Setup** — formation-related documents and initial financial organization.
- **Document Review** — structured review before the appropriate next step is taken.

Service scope depends on the client’s facts, documents, jurisdiction, and requested work.

## Who we support

- U.S. small businesses and business owners;
- clients who need better-organized records and filing information;
- remote service requests across the United States;
- English- and Russian-speaking clients.

## How the process starts

1. Submit a [structured request](https://financialstreamllc.com/contact/#structured-request).
2. Provide the relevant details or documents requested by the form.
3. Financial Stream reviews the situation and requested scope.
4. The client receives an appropriate next step.
