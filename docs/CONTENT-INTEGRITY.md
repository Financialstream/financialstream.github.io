# Financial Stream Content Integrity

## Objective

Financial Stream content must build trust through accurate scope, clear qualifications, and verifiable claims. Marketing language must never outrun the underlying service, evidence, or professional review.

## Approved core service framing

The approved production service structure contains six equal core service areas:

1. Bookkeeping / QuickBooks
2. Tax Returns / Tax Preparation
3. Sales Tax / DOR Reporting
4. Payroll / L&I / Quarterly Reporting
5. Business Setup / Company Formation
6. Financial Consulting / Document Review

Payroll is a confirmed, approved core Financial Stream service. It does not require renewed owner confirmation before normal maintenance, documentation, navigation, or content work.

Do not:

- remove Payroll from the approved service structure;
- demote Payroll to an optional, unconfirmed, or secondary service;
- ask the owner to reconfirm whether Payroll is offered merely because an older document omitted it;
- separate Financial Consulting from Document Review without explicit owner approval.

Do not add a seventh service category or materially change the six approved service scopes without explicit approval.

## Prohibited claims

Do not publish:

- fake ratings or fabricated review counts;
- invented testimonials;
- guaranteed refunds, savings, approvals, outcomes, rankings, leads, or revenue;
- guaranteed penalty avoidance or audit defense;
- claims that a consultation is free or time-limited unless currently approved and operationally true;
- invented certifications, licenses, awards, partnerships, team size, office locations, or years of experience;
- protected-portal or security claims that have not been technically verified;
- claims that AI or automation replaces professional judgment;
- urgency language designed to manufacture a deadline that does not exist.

## Metrics and evidence

Any number used publicly must have:

1. a defined source;
2. a defined time period;
3. a reproducible calculation or preserved snapshot;
4. wording that does not imply a broader result than the evidence supports.

Website traffic, impressions, indexed pages, completed filings, client counts, and similar metrics must not be converted into unsupported claims about leads, conversions, revenue, savings, or future performance.

## Professional-information boundary

General website and article content should not be framed as individualized accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice.

Use language such as:

- “may depend on your facts and jurisdiction”;
- “documents and scope are reviewed before the next step”;
- “general information only”;
- “professional review may be required.”

Avoid absolute statements where rules differ by date, entity type, location, filing status, agency interpretation, or client circumstances.

## Date-sensitive content

Tax and regulatory content must identify the applicable year or effective date when relevant.

Before updating date-sensitive material:

- verify the current primary source;
- distinguish enacted rules from proposals;
- update page dates transparently;
- avoid silently carrying forward old thresholds or deadlines;
- preserve historical context where an older article remains live.

## Testimonials and social proof

Use only authentic, approved testimonials. Preserve the original meaning and do not strengthen the client’s statement through editing.

Do not:

- create composite clients;
- invent names, roles, or locations;
- imply independent verification where none exists;
- use a testimonial for a service the reviewer did not receive.

## Localization integrity

English and Russian versions should communicate the same material service scope, qualifications, and limitations.

Localization may adapt phrasing for natural communication, but it must not:

- add a stronger promise in one language;
- omit a material limitation;
- change who the service is for;
- change a deadline, amount, agency, or jurisdiction;
- turn general information into individualized advice.

## ProAI Expert attribution

Approved neutral attribution:

> Website and digital systems designed and developed by ProAI Expert.

The attribution must not imply that ProAI Expert provides accounting or tax services. Financial Stream remains the client-facing professional-services brand.

## Review checklist

Before merging public-content changes, confirm:

- [ ] service scope matches the six approved core service areas;
- [ ] Payroll remains present as an equal approved core service where the complete service structure is represented;
- [ ] facts and dates are verified;
- [ ] no unsupported guarantee is present;
- [ ] no fabricated social proof is present;
- [ ] professional-information boundaries are clear;
- [ ] both language versions remain materially consistent;
- [ ] contact and consent language remains accurate;
- [ ] developer attribution does not dominate the client brand.
