Overview

Information about service-related SMS communication, consent, opt-out, help instructions, and contact options.

SMS Consent

By calling our business number or submitting a contact form with the SMS checkbox selected, you consent to receive text messages related to your inquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Types of Messages

  • Missed-call follow-up when you call our business number and we miss your call.
  • One-to-one communication to answer questions or coordinate an appointment after you reply or opt in.
  • Service-related reminders or updates connected to your inquiry.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your request, appointment coordination, and service-related communication needs.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact Financial Stream LLC by phone or email.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.
