Financial Stream answer

Do you work remotely across the U.S.?

Yes. Financial Stream supports clients remotely across the U.S. For Washington businesses, DOR, L&I, payroll, and Sales Tax context can also be reviewed when applicable.

Financial Stream answer

Do I need every document ready before I contact you?

No. Share what you already have. Missing records, access, notices, or prior documents can be clarified after the request.

Financial Stream answer

Can I start if I am not sure which service I need?

Yes. Describe the situation, and Financial Stream can help identify whether the next step is bookkeeping, cleanup, payroll, Sales Tax, tax preparation, business setup, or document review.

Financial Stream answer

Can several services be connected?

Yes. Bookkeeping, payroll records, Sales Tax / DOR, tax preparation, and document review often connect. The request helps clarify the practical order of work.

Financial Stream answer

Can you review QuickBooks, payroll, Sales Tax, DOR, L&I, or tax documents?

Yes. Financial Stream can review the available context and clarify what is needed for the next step. The exact scope depends on the records, deadlines, and task.
