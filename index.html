How we work

A clear process from structured request to next step.

We start with context, clarify documents or access, define the scope, and move into the right work only after the practical path is clear.

  1. 01

    Send a structured request

    Share your situation, business type, available documents, and the area where you need help.

  2. 02

    We review the context

    Financial Stream LLC looks at the request and identifies whether the next step is bookkeeping, cleanup, tax preparation, payroll, Sales Tax / DOR, or document review.

  3. 03

    Documents or access are clarified

    Depending on the request, we may ask for QuickBooks access, bank statements, payroll records, tax notices, prior returns, or agency documents.

  4. 04

    Scope and next step are defined

    Before work begins, the likely scope, missing information, and next practical step are clarified.

  5. 05

    Work begins with a clear path

    Once the needed information is available, work can move into bookkeeping, filing preparation, cleanup, reporting, or document support.
