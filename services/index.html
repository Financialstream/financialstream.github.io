Before you contact us

What to prepare before you send a request

You do not need to have everything perfect before contacting us, but a few details help us understand the next step faster.

  • state and business type
  • business name and entity type
  • QuickBooks access or bookkeeping status
  • bank or credit card statements if cleanup is needed
  • IRS, DOR, or L&I notices
  • payroll records or payroll reports
  • Sales Tax / DOR documents
  • prior tax returns
  • deadlines or urgent timing
  • a short explanation of what feels unclear
