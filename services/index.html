What helps us route your request

A few details help route the request.

You do not need everything ready before reaching out. Start with what you already have, then Financial Stream can clarify what is useful next.

Entity or business details

Business name, state, entity type, owners, EIN if available, and a short activity summary.

Financial access or records

QuickBooks status, recent reports, bank statements, payroll records, or Sales Tax / DOR records.

Recent notices or tax documents

IRS, DOR, or L&I notices, prior returns, deadlines, and document requests.

Do not send sensitive passwords or full SSNs through unsecured messages.
